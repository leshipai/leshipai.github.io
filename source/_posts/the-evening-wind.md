The evening wind takes its time with the shirt on the balcony line, as if it were counting the things I have not finished.

<!-- more -->

> The road bends and then it bends again,
> but by the time you come around it
> you are no longer the one who set out.

## Autumn

The persimmons go red on the branch and nobody is in any hurry to pick them.

It gets dark a little earlier every day, and the lamps come on a little earlier every day too.

Written out, a poem looks like taking one useless sentence and putting it carefully on its own lines.
