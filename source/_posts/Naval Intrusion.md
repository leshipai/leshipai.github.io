### Description

We are provided with the challenge image:

![](images/ship-image-01.png)

We need to  ` identify the original make and model of the vessel.`

`Answer Format: type-123q-nato-reporting-name
`Answer Example: type-555d-kingkong-IV`

### Solve

Reverse image Search returns various results and of them a [site](https://www.naval-technology.com/projects/type-054a-jiangkai-ii-class-frigate/) with a matching image and information on the vessel.
![](images/erfwdfdsf.png)

Answer -> `type-054a-jiangkai-II`
